# Why there is no front end

A flight school runs on a syllabus, a booking sheet, a certificate list and a ledger. Those are tables. What a school pays FlightLogger for, on top of them, is the layer that lets people who do not write queries get at them: the booking calendar, the student pages, the dashboards.

That layer used to be the whole product, because talking to a database was hard. It is not hard any more. Open this folder in Claude Code, ask "who is booked solo this week and can they all go?", and it runs the query and explains the answer.

## What you gain

- **Better answers.** A dashboard shows what the vendor chose to chart. Here the CFI asks their own question against their own records: which students fly most often and progress fastest, which instructor's students repeat circuits, what weather cost last quarter.
- **No fee per student.** The bill does not grow because you enrolled a bigger intake.
- **Your records in your database.** Training records are yours to keep for as long as your exposition says. Back them up, query them from anything, keep them when you change systems.
- **A process that matches your exposition.** Your syllabus, your solo rules, your limits. When the exposition changes, the rule changes with it.

## What a screen gives that this does not

- **Students booking themselves.** There is no online booking calendar for students here. The office books by asking. Enterprise DNA builds a booking page on top of these records for schools that want one.
- **A phone in the aircraft.** Instructors log and debrief at a computer, or by asking from a phone with Claude Code. There is no offline app.
- **Drag and drop.** Moving a booking is a sentence, not a drag across the calendar.
- **Student logins.** Students do not sign in to see their own record. Progress notes and statements are drafted for the office to send.

## Who this fits

Schools and clubs whose CFI and office would rather ask than click, and who want the training records in a database they own. If your students need to book themselves tonight, run it with a booking page on top, or keep FlightLogger for the calendar and use this for the records and the questions.
